THE PROJECT

Complex risks.
A connected response.

Disasters rarely happen in isolation. A flood can disrupt power, transport and essential services. Effective preparedness means understanding these connections and acting together.

RESILIACT develops an integrated web-based platform to help authorities anticipate the impacts of interacting hazards, use early warnings and coordinate decisions across organisations and sectors.

Its full name captures that ambition: Cross-regional action for enhanced impact forecasting, early warning, and resilient governance.

THE RESILIACT PLATFORM

Five components.
One integrated platform.

The RESILIACT Platform is being developed to connect harmonised data, interacting hazards, impact forecasts, decision support and accessible risk communication.

MSDIF

A common foundation for shared data.

The Multi-System Data Interoperability Framework harmonises data exchange across hazard, exposure and vulnerability domains. It addresses the technical, semantic and governance requirements of working across systems.

What this enables

Organisations can connect relevant information and use it together to support forecasting and decisions.

Co-created and evaluated through Living Labs.
